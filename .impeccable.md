## Design Context

### Users

The site serves donors, supporters, potential partners, volunteers, community members, veterans, beneficiaries, and families. Visitors need to understand what ГО "Побратими разом" does, see proof of real activity, and take action through support, partnership, volunteering, or direct contact.

### Brand Personality

The interface should feel institutional, human, field-tested, veteran-rooted, civilian-accessible, resolute, and practical. It should acknowledge Ukraine's full-scale wartime context directly and respectfully.

### Aesthetic Direction

The design should avoid generic charity softness, corporate NGO blandness, and performative military decoration. Use strong typography, real photography, editorial layouts, restrained Ukrainian identity cues, and controlled military-adjacent symbolism. The refreshed identity should preserve the human support/brotherhood concept from the current logo while simplifying it into a more usable mark.

Color direction: deep ink/navy, muted field green/olive, warm off-white paper surfaces, restrained Ukrainian blue/yellow accents, and one clear signal color for urgent support moments.

### Design Principles

- Lead with credibility and a clear `Підтримати` action.
- Let real organization media carry trust wherever possible.
- Keep layouts structured and polished; editors control content, not arbitrary page composition.
- Use military cues with discipline and restraint, never as empty decoration.
- Build for accessibility, Ukrainian-first content, and easy long-term maintenance.
